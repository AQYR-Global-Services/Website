Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how AQYR Global Services handles information when you visit this website or contact our team.

Optional website analytics

If you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits, successful enquiry submissions, general device information and broad traffic sources. Google may use first-party analytics cookies and process technical data outside Australia. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our integration. We do not load the Google Analytics tag before you accept.

Our reporting excludes URL query strings and fragments. An enquiry counts only after the sending service accepts it; it does not mean the team has replied or that it reached the inbox. An opaque receipt is kept only in the current tab to avoid counting retries and is never sent to analytics. We do not send your name, contact details, enquiry text, calculator answers or points breakdown to analytics. You can accept, decline or withdraw your choice through Analytics preferences in the footer; declining does not restrict access to this website. Your preference is stored locally for up to 180 days. Withdrawal stops future collection but does not erase data previously collected.

Who we are

“AQYR Global Services”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to AQYR GLOBAL SERVICES PTY LTD (ABN 23 619 566 040), which provides the services described on this website. For privacy enquiries, contact us at info@hiremiiglobal.com.

Information you choose to provide

If you make an enquiry, you may provide details such as your name, email address, phone number, company or occupation, the type of help you are seeking, and information you choose to include in your message. Please do not send passports, identity documents, health information or other sensitive records through a general website enquiry.

How the enquiry form works

When you click Submit enquiry, the website sends your selected form details to our server and through Resend to info@hiremiiglobal.com. A reply-to address lets our team respond to your email. If you choose a calculator estimate or state assessment, those selected answers are included for review as your self-assessment. These details are sent only when you submit the form. If submission is unavailable, you can contact the team directly using the displayed email address.

We use enquiry information to respond, understand the assistance requested, assess whether our services may be suitable, communicate with you and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use this website enquiry form to request sensitive identity or immigration documents.

Information collected when you visit

Our hosting, security and content-delivery services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as an IP address, browser or device details, pages requested and request times. Your chosen URL determines the page language. When you switch calculator language in the same tab, scoring selections are temporarily held in that tab’s session storage, read and removed on arrival, and not restored after five minutes. Free-text searches and enquiry details are excluded; this handoff is not sent to analytics.

The logo, team photographs and font files are served from this website. On the contact page, Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical browser and request signals to help prevent automated abuse. It is separate from optional analytics. Our server validates submissions and limits request size; hosting security may also apply request-frequency limits.

Disclosure, storage and access

We use Vercel for website hosting and submission security, Resend for enquiry email delivery, and our email provider to receive and respond. Those providers may process or retain information in Australia or other countries. This form does not create a customer record in our CRM or add you to a marketing list. Enquiries remain in our email systems and relevant provider delivery records; retention depends on operational needs and applicable requirements. Please contact us about access, correction or deletion requests.

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information you have provided, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Contact info@hiremiiglobal.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For questions about this policy, email info@hiremiiglobal.com.
