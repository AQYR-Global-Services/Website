AQYR GLOBAL SERVICES

Company profile.

Australian visa, migration and workforce support. A subsidiary of AQYR, with service roots dating back to 2017.

Who we are

AQYR Global Services brings Australian visa advice, employer sponsorship and workforce support together. We work with businesses seeking suitable talent and individuals planning work, study, family life or a visit to Australia.

482 and 186 Direct Entry employer sponsorship are core areas of our work. We also assist with visitor, student, skilled, partner and other visa matters, tailoring the scope to each client’s background and plans.

Our listed-group background

AQYR Global Services is a subsidiary of AQYR. AQYR is the group brand, and the group’s ASX-listed entity is Hiremii Limited (ASX:HMI). Within that wider group, our work focuses on visa, migration and workforce mobility services.

The wider group brings together specialist recruitment experience and workforce intelligence technology. AQYR Global Services shares that group background and access to its wider resources, while our own work focuses on visa advice, employer sponsorship and individual case preparation.

When needed, we can connect a staffing enquiry with the group’s recruitment experience. Additional work is agreed separately; migration advice and evidence review stay with the team handling your case.

Group listing and investor disclosures relate to Hiremii Limited; AQYR Global Services is not a separately listed company. Migration advice and case assessment remain the responsibility of the professional team.

Our service history

Our service roots include Prince Migration, established in 2017. The business developed experience across migration advice, education-related matters and employer sponsorship, working with individual applicants and corporate clients.

In 2025, Hiremii announced the acquisition of Prince Migration to expand its migration and talent mobility offering. Our predecessor service brand, Hiremii Global Services, brought that migration experience into a broader workforce-services setting. We now present these services under the AQYR Global Services brand.

Experience with employers

Our team has handled more than 200 employer-sponsored cases across 482 and 186. We review why the business needs a role, what it involves and how the applicant’s experience fits, before organising the nomination and visa evidence.

We help clarify workforce needs, review the proposed role and relevant employment information, and coordinate case preparation. For businesses establishing or expanding Australian operations, agreed support can also include HR preparation, employment records, onboarding arrangements and communication with local teams.

Previous cases inform how we identify and prepare for potential issues; they do not determine the outcome of a new application. Each business and applicant is assessed on their own circumstances.

Support for individuals

We start with your visa history, occupation, qualifications, family and plans. Then we explain the relevant options and agree what needs to be prepared.

Where relevant, that preparation may include skills assessment information, qualification recognition and the connection between education, training and future employment. The scope is agreed with you, without promises of a job, an assessment result or a visa outcome.

Professional people, individual cases

Migration advice, legal support, document preparation and client communication have distinct roles in our team. We agree the work with you, then review the records against your circumstances.
