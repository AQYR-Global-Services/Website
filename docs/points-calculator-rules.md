# Skilled migration calculator rule snapshot

Base points checked on 15 October 2026; English evidence and eight-state nomination review on 22 October 2026. This is a planning tool, not a visa eligibility engine. All eight states/territories offer 189/190/491 estimates: VIC190/491 retain VPT only; every 189 and the other seven 190 selections use federal EOI. Scoring inputs are directly available; state-specific nomination guidance is optional. ACT and Tasmania have explicitly separate nomination self-assessments, never added to EOI and not carried in the enquiry score. VPT qualification and partner answers stay separate from federal answers. No invitation forecasts, implied government affiliation or invented state weighting formula.

## Federal model

Age bands: 18–24 = 25; 25–32 = 30; 33–39 = 25; 40–44 = 15. Age 45+ blocks a qualifying total. The supported input range begins at 18.

English Competent / Proficient / Superior = 0 / 10 / 20; below Competent blocks a qualifying total. `public/english-rules.mjs` supports IELTS Academic/General Training, PTE Academic, Cambridge C1 Advanced, CELPIP General, Michigan English Test, OET, LANGUAGECERT Academic and TOEFL iBT. The UI displays the selected test's L/R/W/S thresholds directly from the scoring table. Non-IELTS tests require a real, non-future date; 7 August 2025 selects new/legacy rules. CELPIP, MET and LANGUAGECERT are unavailable before that date. MET has no published Superior threshold and never awards it, even at maximum scores. Legacy OET accepts report letter grades (B = Competent/Proficient; A = Superior); current OET uses component numeric thresholds in increments of 10. TOEFL's 26 July 2023–4 May 2024 exclusion is enforced. New-table TOEFL requires an explicit Australian registration/0–30 scale confirmation; 1–6 scores are not converted. C1 Advanced from 12 February 2024 through 6 August 2025 requires paper-format confirmation. Other retakes and visa-specific evidence conditions still need manual checking. UK/US/Canada/NZ/Republic of Ireland citizenship plus valid passport can establish Competent only; a passport selection cannot claim Proficient/Superior. All accepted test-centre exams are listed; remote/at-home tests are excluded. Test validity is stated as 3 years before visa invitation for 189/190 but is not automatically assessed because the tool does not collect an invitation date.

Employment in preceding ten years: overseas <3 / 3–<5 / 5–<8 / 8+ years = 0/5/10/15; Australian <1 / 1–<3 / 3–<5 / 5–<8 / 8+ = 0/5/10/15/20. Combined cap 20. No inference of assessed skilled dates or verification of overlapping employment. Eligible paid work requires 20 hours/week and the nominated/closely related occupation; Australian visa conditions also apply.

Highest recognised qualification only: PhD20; Bachelor/Honours/Masters15; Australian diploma/trade or suitable assessing-authority award10; other0. Specialist Australian research Masters/PhD in specified fields10; Australian study5; eligible regional study5 (requires Australian study); eligible Professional Year5; qualifying NAATI community-language credential5. Inputs explain the qualifying conditions. Regional and specialist claims are disabled when their prerequisite selections are inconsistent.

Partner: single/Australian citizen or PR partner10; qualifying skilled partner10; qualifying English-only partner5; none0. Skilled partner conditions are shown beside the field. No double counting between categories.

NSW190 displays base +5 as a **conditional nomination scenario**, not an existing nomination. VIC190 is VPT-only and has no nomination bonus or federal result. 189 never adds nomination points, regardless of chosen state. Salary does not affect federal points. 65 is a federal minimum threshold only, not an invitation cutoff.

## Victoria VPT model

Age18=265,19=282,20–29=300,30=286,31=270,32=256,33=240,34=226,35=210,36=196,37=180,38=166,39=150,40=136,41=106,42=76,43=46,44=16.

English0/100/200. Nominated-occupation experience0/1/2–3/4–5/6–7/8+ years = 0/20/40/60/80/100. The official table currently says “More than 8 years” and omits exactly eight. At the site owner's request, this planning tool groups exactly eight with the 100-point band. The field hint, result assumptions and enquiry answer identify this as an estimate rather than a confirmed government interpretation. The dropdown uses value `8`; legacy value `9` remains accepted for existing enquiry links. EOI totals are unaffected.

Education Other25, Bachelor/Honours50, Masters75, PhD100. Nominated ANZSCO skill level1=50; levels2/3=0. The occupation picker derives the level only from an explicitly selected six-digit official occupation record, not from a guessed title or code prefix. It uses ABS ANZSCO **2013 v1.2**, the version applicable to 189/190 (not 2022 or OSCA): Structure Table 5 supplies 1,023 occupations and levels; Index Table 2 supplies 2,213 alternative names/specialisations. Common Chinese search aids are editorial, not official translations. Users must select their assessed occupation, and a match does not establish visa eligibility. Unknown codes and levels4/5 withhold the VPT total. Source URLs, attribution and SHA256 hashes are recorded in `assets/anzsco-source.json`.

Partner50 only with the federal **skilled partner** category (not single/AU partner), inclusion in the same nominated application and a six-digit partner code in the published Victorian priority list. The snapshot includes every published code from healthcare, social assistance, education and construction. This membership check is not an occupation eligibility determination or promise of a priority-stream invitation. Updates to the priority list require updating the Set and regression tests.

Current eligible Victorian annual earnings: <$80k=0; 5k bands from $80k have points 8,16,24,32,39,47,55,63,71,79,87,95,103,111,118,126,134,142; $170k+=150. Users enter the **official earnings estimator result**, not arbitrary gross income: current Victorian residence, skilled employment for a physically Victorian employer (no virtual office), highest-paying eligible job only, excluding super and other excluded payments. Casual loading needs the official adjustment. The job can differ from nominated occupation. Salary is ignored when claim eligibility is No or the mode is not VIC190.

VPT maximum950 is a theoretical total, not a pass mark. State and federal eligibility remain separate; federal and VPT totals must never be combined. Age at ROI may differ from age at federal invitation: the form tells users to recalculate each scenario if needed.

## State and territory nomination systems

All 189 selections use the same federal table and no nomination bonus. All federal 190 estimates include a clearly conditional +5, not an existing nomination or state ranking score. State-nominated 491 estimates use conditional +15 and require acknowledgement of regional obligations; no state ranking is calculated for491. State eligibility, occupation lists, residence, job offers, stream rules and program opening status are not inferred from EOI totals.

- VIC: [VPT](https://liveinmelbourne.vic.gov.au/migrate/skilled-migration-visas/vpt), preserved as the existing independent 950-point model.
- NSW: [190 nomination](https://www.nsw.gov.au/visas-and-migration/skilled-visas/skilled-nominated-visa-subclass-190), selection from EOI; no invented state numeric weighting.
- QLD: [skilled pathways](https://www.migration.qld.gov.au/visa-options/skilled-visas), different local/offshore/graduate/construction streams; regional small-business 491 is not treated as a 190 pathway.
- WA: [SNMP](https://migration.wa.gov.au/our-services-support/state-nominated-migration-program), General/Graduate streams, occupation lists and ordered invitation priorities, not an additive state points table. Browser-read current page still describes 2025–26 criteria; no 2026–27 opening claim is encoded.
- SA: [skilled migration](https://migration.sa.gov.au/before-applying/visa-options-and-pathways/skilled-migrants), stream-specific requirements, onshore ROI/offshore EOI selection. No invented state numeric score.
- NT: [eligibility](https://australiasnorthernterritory.com.au/move/migrate-to-work/nt-government-visa-nomination/eligibility), residence/graduate/offshore conditions and settlement commitment; no invented separate additive score or open-program claim.
- ACT: [resident Matrix](https://www.act.gov.au/migration/skilled-migrants/canberra-matrix/check-your-canberra-matrix-score-canberra-residents), [overseas Matrix](https://www.act.gov.au/migration/skilled-migrants/canberra-matrix/check-your-canberra-matrix-score-overseas-applicants). Optional local self-assessment uses each official category, one selection per category, with full-rule/evidence confirmation. Matrix points do not establish eligibility, and no invitation cutoff is encoded.
- TAS: [TSE](https://www.migration.tas.gov.au/skilled_migration/skilled-workers-and-graduates-living-in-Tasmania/tasmanian-skilled-employment-pathways-tse), [TSG](https://www.migration.tas.gov.au/skilled_migration/skilled-workers-and-graduates-living-in-Tasmania/tasmanian-skilled-graduate-pathways-tsg), [TER](https://www.migration.tas.gov.au/skilled_migration/skilled-workers-and-graduates-living-in-Tasmania/tasmanian-established-resident-pathways-ter). Optional 190 attribute estimates group mutually exclusive employment, salary, residence and PhD bands, with a separate priority tier. These are self-declared claims, not a nomination or eligibility assessment. Every claim requires checking the linked full conditions, occupational caveats and one-current-job rule. [Offshore health/education job-offer 190](https://www.migration.tas.gov.au/skilled_migration/skilled-workers-living-overseas/subclass-190-overseas-applicant-health-or-education-sector-job-offer-pathway) is linked separately: the official pathway awards a Gold Pass to qualifying ROIs; no arbitrary numeric points are invented.

The 190 interface no longer requires a nomination pathway selection before scoring. A native optional disclosure provides 23 pathway guides across eight states. Qualitative streams show a read-only explanation and requirements list, with no professional-condition self-check dropdowns or completion counts. These guides are collapsed by default below the scoring inputs. ACT Matrix and TAS numeric ranking tools stay expanded by default; actual scoring inputs, missing-answer links and conflict checks remain. Their evidence acknowledgement uses plain language rather than asking visitors to certify all official rules. A short visible reminder distinguishes points estimates from nomination eligibility. `public/nomination-pathways.mjs` holds editorial guidance, not exhaustive legal criteria. QLD/WA/SA notes retain applicable-intake cautions; no policy requirements or scoring weights change. NT offshore is not offered as an ordinary 190 pathway. VIC offshore resets the earnings claim to No.

Federal employment/education/partner inputs remain directly visible, with EOI independently displayed in the result column. Shared age/English remain directly visible in every state/visa mode. VIC190/491 use the official VPT-only ranking model. No nomination-path selection or qualitative self-check is required to access any scoring input. Local answers are unnamed controls, excluded from ordinary form values. They are included in an enquiry fragment only when the separate state nomination/ROI attachment is explicitly selected. They reset on state/visa/pathway change or form reset, while language switching preserves answers. `public/nomination-ranking.mjs` does not persist answers; the controller only transfers whitelisted answers after opt-in. VIC491 uses the official shared VPT factors; other491 modes use the separate federal regional scenario described below, without reusing ACT/TAS190 state tables.

## NSW / program status

NSW's page describes multiple selection factors and ANZSCO-unit-group ranking but no separate numerical weights. Show federal EOI only. Do not create a NSW points test or score salary. The live page currently contains a closure/review notice; link users to its current status rather than encode an opening date or imply nomination is open. No fees, allocations or historical cutoffs are used in either calculator.

## Maintenance and testing

### 491 regional scenario (reviewed 22 October 2026)

Victoria 491 now uses the official VPT model, independently of federal EOI. Verified on 23 October 2026: the official VPT page expressly applies its seven factors to both 190 and 491 ROI ranking. VIC491 has a maximum950, never VPT+15. Regional postcode and acknowledgement checks remain required. Other states retain the **state-nominated federal EOI scenario**: conditional nomination adds **15**, never 5+15; maximum145. ACT/TAS491 do not reuse their 190 ranking tables. No family sponsorship or nomination eligibility decision is made.

WA/SA/TAS/ACT/NT are designated regional throughout, including their capitals. The UI links each state's nomination source and the federal postcode table. Exact street addresses are not collected or validated. The inline postcode lookup compares the official regional ranges against ABS geographic postcode references; details are below. State-specific nomination and settlement conditions can be narrower than federal regional designation.

491 requires a recognised, non-cross-border regional postcode matching the selected state plus an explicit regional-obligations acknowledgement before any total or enquiry snapshot is produced. This is not evidence of eligibility. State or visa changes reset the acknowledgement; postcode results immediately re-evaluate against the new state. On opt-in the enquiry snapshot carries the postcode and acknowledgement but no street address. The ordinary regional-study +5 remains independent and is not awarded automatically for choosing491. Contact imports support all24 state/visa combinations: VIC491 imports VPT up to950 without nomination+15, while other491 modes enforce145 and label conditional+15. VIC491 snapshots use version2/model vpt; legacy EOI links require recalculation rather than being relabelled as VPT. No automatic messages or new data storage.

State selection is a left-hand eight-state index on desktop and a compact four-column switcher on smaller screens, not a state dropdown. A hidden named state control preserves scoring/enquiry compatibility. The default view is VIC190; the main region shows the selected state's calculator with three native radio cards for 189/190/491. Choosing an index entry updates the selected-state heading, rules, pathways and score cards, then returns focus to the calculator heading. Common federal answers retain the existing switching behaviour; local ranking answers reset on state changes. Reset clears answers while retaining the selected state and visa context. No new routes, storage or scoring changes are introduced.

The right-hand result summary adapts to the selected numeric model. ACT190 Matrix and TAS190 TSE/TSG/TER show two large, independent scores: the selected state assessment first and federal EOI second. VIC190/491 retain one VPT card; every189, other-state190 and TAS offshore guidance retain one federal card. Missing, unconfirmed or conflicting state answers show a dash and a review link, never an old total/tier. State/visa/path changes and Reset clear the state card as appropriate. Two independent enquiry checkboxes attach completed federal EOI/VIC VPT and/or state nomination/ROI context and answers. ROI-only consultations remain possible when EOI is incomplete. Contact imports revalidate and recompute snapshots, show independent previews and let visitors opt out of either attachment. Incomplete, unconfirmed or conflicting state claims are labelled and never given a total; non-numeric streams provide pathway context only. No totals or eligibility rules change, and scores are never added together.

The points breakdown is a permanently expanded, labelled section rather than an accordion. Its itemised rows remain visible across state, visa and language changes and reset; only assumptions/important notes remain collapsible.

ACT/TAS numeric assessments report all unanswered categories, invalid selections and outstanding evidence confirmation independently of conflicts. Blank is not silently treated as zero: users can explicitly select No verified claim / not applicable. Checklists without a numeric model are not labelled as missing score inputs. Federal EOI/VPT retain their existing required-item summaries.

Employment options carry explicit relationship metadata rather than relying on point values. `rankingConflicts` runs before completion/evidence confirmation; a conflict withholds both total and tier. The interface preserves the selections, highlights both fields, and explains the contradiction in either language. Correcting either answer restores calculation. Explicitly unrelated employment, partner work and a future graduate-program offer alone are not treated as current related employment. This targeted validation does not certify that every other eligibility condition or combination has been satisfied.

### Inline postcode lookup and shared English input

The primary491 external link row was replaced with a four-digit local postcode input, a Check button/Enter shortcut and an accessible live result. Home Affairs ranges are implemented in `public/regional-postcodes.mjs`; `public/postcode-reference.mjs` contains2642 geographic codes from ABS ASGS2026 POA, excluding9494/9797/ZZZZ. Unknown/new/PO-box/special codes are **unknown**, not automatically non-regional or regional. Fifteen ABS cross-border codes return manual-check status rather than inferring an address. Other Territories are outside the eight-state calculators. Recognised non-regional, mismatched-state, ambiguous or unknown inputs withhold491 totals and snapshots. Source metadata: `assets/regional-postcodes-source.json`. No maps/geocoders/external queries run during lookup.

The shared Age & English section is outside the collapsed federal panel in all24 state/subclass modes. Test options appear first (all8 accepted exams), with date/evidence branches and automated per-component grading unchanged; manual verified levels remain a secondary last option. ACT resident/overseas and TAS190 local English controls are derived from that same input, not separately guessed: ACT Superior15/Proficient10/Competent0; TAS Proficient/Superior or qualifying passport exemption1, otherwise Competent0. Missing, incomplete or below-Competent English clears the derived selection, never retaining a stale score. Local reset/path/language handling preserves this single source. No partner-English or other distinct eligibility assumptions are inferred. Pending-English links return to the actual test input. Component thresholds, date exclusions and scoring tables have not changed.
