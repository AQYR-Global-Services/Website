03Skilled employment

Federal points: eligible paid work in the nominated or a closely related occupation in the 10 years before invitation, at least 20 hours/week. Australian work also requires an eligible visa and compliance with its conditions. Do not double-count overlapping periods; check assessed / deemed-skilled dates. Combined employment points are capped at 20.

04Education & additional claims
Highest qualification only. Overseas degrees must meet the recognised standard. “Doctor” as a professional title alone does not qualify as a PhD.

05Partner circumstances
Skilled partner: included in the same visa subclass, not an Australian citizen/PR, under 45 at invitation, Competent English, occupation on the same eligible list, and a suitable non-485 skills assessment. English-only partner must also be included and not be an Australian citizen/PR. Relationship changes may affect points.

      RULES & SOURCES

      Know what your score means.

      Base scoring: 15 October 2026; English and state nomination review: 22 October 2026. Policies can change; check the official sources before making a claim.

      189 / 190 / 491

      The same federal factors apply across all Australian states and territories. A 190 nomination adds 5 points; a state-nominated 491 scenario adds 15, conditional on effective nomination. These bonuses are not combined. 189 has no state nomination points. Scores do not verify occupation eligibility, assessments, health, character or other requirements.

      Victoria · VPT

      Seven weighted factors rank Victorian 190 and 491 ROIs. VPT is separate from SkillSelect and has no fixed pass mark. This tool does not predict invitations or confirm a priority stream. Use the current official occupation list and nomination criteria.

      NSW · selection, not a VPT

      NSW considers occupation-group ranking and multiple factors. Its published guidance does not provide a separate numerical weighting table equivalent to VPT. We therefore show federal points only, not a made-up NSW score. Check current program availability and eligibility.
