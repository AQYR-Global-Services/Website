VISA SERVICES

More than work visas.

Choose a visa topic below.

Visitor visasVisit Australia for travel, family or friends.

When to get in touch

Planning a holiday, visiting family or friends, or unsure which visitor option fits your passport and current location? We can also discuss previous refusals or changes to your travel plans.

How we can help

We clarify the purpose and timing of the visit, review the visa option and supporting evidence, and assist with preparation and lodgement within the agreed scope. Visitor visas are for temporary visits, not a route to working in Australia.

Before our first conversation

Your nationality, current location and visa status, intended travel dates, who you will visit, how the trip will be funded, and any relevant visa history. A short summary is enough to begin.

Student visasPlan a course and the evidence for your application.

When to get in touch

Starting a course in Australia, planning further study, or reviewing how a new course fits your background? We discuss your study goals alongside your current visa and timing.

How we can help

We organise enrolment records (CoE), review Genuine Student evidence and check financial, English and health-insurance requirements. Your study plans need consistent evidence of your circumstances.

Before our first conversation

Your education and work history, proposed course and provider, offer or CoE if available, English results, current visa details, funding arrangements and any accompanying family plans.

Skilled migrationReview your occupation, skills and points claims.

When to get in touch

Considering a points-tested pathway such as 189, 190 or 491? We review your occupation and background, including whether state nomination or a regional pathway is relevant.

How we can help

We review occupation and skills-assessment requirements, English evidence and the basis of points claims. We can assist with an Expression of Interest, relevant nomination preparation and a visa application if invited, as agreed. Meeting the minimum points threshold does not guarantee an invitation; state criteria also vary.

Before our first conversation

Your age, occupation, qualifications, employment dates and duties, English results, skills-assessment status, current visa, partner details where relevant, and any EOI or nomination already submitted.

Partner & family visasPrepare for joining a partner or family in Australia.

When to get in touch

Planning to join a spouse or de facto partner who is an Australian citizen, permanent resident or eligible New Zealand citizen? We also welcome parent, child and other family enquiries, which require separate assessment.

How we can help

For partner matters, we review the relationship history, sponsorship circumstances and whether an onshore or offshore pathway is relevant. We help organise genuine relationship evidence and clarify the application stages. Other family visas have their own sponsorship, eligibility and waiting arrangements.

Before our first conversation

Each person’s citizenship or visa status and location, key relationship or family dates, any previous sponsorships or applications, and your plans for living together. You do not need to email private relationship evidence at the first enquiry.

BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

A short overview is enough.

A brief background and your goal are enough to start. Please leave out sensitive documents.

Can I enquire without an employer?

Yes. We can discuss your background and whether there may be suitable employer connections. Introductions depend on available roles and the interests of both parties.

Can you help with a visa unrelated to employment?

Yes. We welcome visitor, student, partner, family and other Australian visa enquiries as well as skilled and sponsored migration matters.

Can you review a complicated situation?

Yes. Start with a short explanation of the issue. We can identify the information needed to assess the matter before discussing a suitable service scope.
