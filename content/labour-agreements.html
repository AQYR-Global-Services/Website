AGREEMENT AND VISA

482, 186 and 494.

Not every agreement covers all three visas.

A labour agreement is not a visa. It sets the employer’s approved arrangements; nomination and visa applications remain separate steps.

482
LABOUR AGREEMENT STREAM

Temporary skilled work

The Labour Agreement stream lets nominated workers work temporarily under the approved agreement. The stay is generally up to four years, subject to its terms; some passport holders have different arrangements.

The agreement determines the covered occupations and any approved concessions. Do not assume Core Skills criteria apply unchanged.

Home Affairs: 482 Labour Agreement
186
LABOUR AGREEMENT STREAM

Permanent residence

A permanent visa through the Labour Agreement stream, where the agreement provides for this pathway and the employer and applicant meet the relevant requirements.

This is separate from Direct Entry and TRT. Any required work period and the applicant’s eligibility must be checked against the agreement and visa rules.

Home Affairs: 186 Labour Agreement
494
LABOUR AGREEMENT STREAM

Regional provisional work

A five-year provisional visa under an agreement that allows 494 nominations. It requires living, working and studying in designated regional areas.

It is not permanent residence. Eligible holders may later apply for 191; holding 494 for at least three years is one requirement, not an automatic grant.

Home Affairs: 494 Labour Agreement

Approval of an agreement does not approve a nomination or grant a visa. Available occupations, concessions and permanent residence arrangements depend on the approved agreement and current law.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Employer questions.

Check the fit before planning the application.

Is this only for large companies?

There is no single size threshold across all labour agreements. The relevant framework, genuine labour need, recruitment evidence and business capacity matter. Our service is focused on substantial, ongoing workforce needs.

Can we enquire from any Australian state?

Yes. We assess enquiries across Australia. That does not mean every pathway is available everywhere: DAMA is tied to its designated area, and 494 has designated regional requirements.

Does an agreement relax English, age or salary requirements?

No. Any concessions depend on the applicable agreement and visa stream and must be expressly available or approved. An agreement is not a general exemption from migration or workplace requirements.

Does it guarantee visas or permanent residence?

No. The agreement, nomination and visa stages are separately assessed. A permanent residence pathway must be available under the relevant arrangements, and the applicant must meet its conditions. We do not promise approval or a fixed processing time.
