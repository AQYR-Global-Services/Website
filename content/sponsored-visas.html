YOUR STARTING POINT

Have an employer, or exploring opportunities?

Different starting points need different preparation.

I have an employer

We review your occupation, work history and visa position together with the employer’s proposed role. Willingness to sponsor is a starting point, not confirmation of eligibility.

Discuss your role and background

I am looking for an employer

Start with your background, skills and goals. Where available roles and your experience align, we can explore employer connections. An introduction, job offer, sponsorship or visa outcome cannot be guaranteed.

PERSONAL PREPARATION

Connect your experience with the role.

Begin with an overview, not a collection of sensitive documents.

Your background and records

Occupation, qualifications, dated work history, skills-assessment or licensing information where relevant, English evidence and current visa circumstances.

Your employer and plans

If you have an employer, outline the role, location and proposed timing. If not, explain the type of work and locations you are considering so the discussion starts from realistic options.

HOW WE SUPPORT YOU

APPLICANT QUESTIONS

Questions about your sponsored pathway.

Check your circumstances before choosing a pathway.

Why assess the role if the employer is willing to sponsor?

The proposed occupation, actual duties and employment terms matter alongside your background. We review them together so the preparation is based on the real role, not its title alone.

Does holding 482 mean I will automatically obtain 186?

No. A later permanent-residence pathway needs a separate assessment of the relevant stream, nomination and applicant requirements.

This is general information, not a complete eligibility assessment. Requirements and exemptions can change; use current Home Affairs guidance and advice on your circumstances.
