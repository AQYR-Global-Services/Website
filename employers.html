Can we discuss a workforce plan before identifying candidates?

Yes. Start with the business or project, the roles needed and your preferred timing. We can discuss local and overseas recruitment considerations and what information a later migration assessment would need.

Can you assist with overseas staff or candidates we have already found?

Yes. You do not need to use our talent introductions first. Share the Australian role, candidate background and proposed arrangements so we can assess the relevant migration and sponsorship questions.

Does an approved application cover all our employer obligations?

An approval is not the end of compliance. Sponsorship obligations, the employee’s visa conditions and Australian workplace requirements are separate matters. We can help identify migration-related obligations and coordinate other questions with the appropriate advisers.

How should we respond to a policy change?

Start by checking whether it affects your roles, candidates, proposed applications or existing arrangements, and when it takes effect. We can explain the relevant migration implications and discuss practical next steps. A change does not necessarily affect every employee or case in the same way.

What should we prepare for a first conversation?

A brief business or project overview, position descriptions, locations, indicative employment terms and timing, plus any available candidate or existing sponsorship information. Sensitive documents can be requested later through an agreed channel.
