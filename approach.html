A PRACTICAL PROCESS

What happens at each stage?

Five steps, with the work agreed before we begin.

01

Initial discussion

Tell us your goal, current circumstances and any deadlines. We clarify the enquiry and explain what information is needed for a closer assessment.

For youShare the background and your priorities.
02

Assessment and scope

Review your background and the evidence available, identify questions that could affect the proposed approach and explain the options. Agree the scope, responsibilities and fees before proceeding.

For youReview and confirm the proposed engagement.
03

Preparation plan

Build the preparation list around your circumstances, not just the visa category. Explain what each record needs to establish and who can provide it, including employers or other parties where relevant.

For youGather the records listed for your matter.
04

Case coordination

Compare the account of your circumstances with the supporting records. Follow up on gaps, conflicting dates or unclear responsibilities, and discuss how to address them before the next stage.

For youCheck factual details and provide missing information.
05

Ongoing communication

Explain relevant progress and requests for further information. If your circumstances change, discuss how that may affect the next steps within the agreed service.

For youTell us about changes to your circumstances.

WORKING WITH OUR TEAM

Clear responsibilities. Better communication.

We prepare and review; you provide accurate records. Authorities decide the outcome.

Understand the reasoning.

Two clients considering the same visa may need different preparation. A change of duties, an employment gap or a different family situation can raise different questions. We explain which details matter in your case and what still needs to be established.

Adjust when circumstances change.

If the role, timeline or personal circumstances change, we revisit the relevant parts of the plan with you. The team reviews the information and discusses the next step, rather than treating the original checklist as a fixed answer.
